### Vet Partnership Strategy

**Why Vets:**
- Trusted authority
- Direct access to concerned pet owners
- Perfect for "health anxiety" persona

**Partnership Model:**
- Free Pro accounts for vets
- Referral fees for app mentions
- Co-branded educational content
- Vet report feature (sends data to vet)

### Influencer Strategy

**Tier 1 (1M+ followers):**
- 2-3 partnerships for launch
- $10K-50K per post
- Brand awareness focus

**Tier 2 (100K-1M):**
- 10-20 ongoing partnerships
- $1K-5K per post
- Conversion focus

**Tier 3 (Micro 10K-100K):**
- 50+ ambassadors
- Product exchange + small fee
- Authenticity, niche audiences
